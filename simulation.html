Audited Research Review Findings:
  1. Elementary Upper Bound: A simple repetition baseline ($x \parallel 1^6 \parallel x^{12}$) reaches $B_E = M - K$ on marked erasures (54 at $K=4$, 78 at $K=6$).
  2. The Deletion Catastrophe: However, BOTH the Reviewer Baseline and Uniform Interleaving suffer complete synchronization collapse ($B_{\text{del}} = 0$) upon even a single unmarked deletion ($b=1$).
  3. The GPC Joint Pareto Frontier: GPC achieves $B_E = 47$ ($K=4$) and $B_E = 67$ ($K=6$) while guaranteeing deletion tolerance up to $B_{\text{del}} = 21$ and $31$ under deterministic $O(M)$ greedy sliding-window recovery!
